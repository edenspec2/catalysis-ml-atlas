On your phone

Open this site on your phone and add it to the home screen. iPhone: Safari, Share, Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome menu, Install app.

To move your reading list and notes, use Export, Back up my shelf, then Restore a backup on the other device. Keep PDFs in the group Zotero collection: this site does not store publisher PDFs.

To suggest a paper for everyone, search for it under Find Papers, open it, and choose Propose for the shared atlas.
