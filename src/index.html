PAPER NOTES

On your phone

Open this site's link on your phone and add it to the home screen.

iPhone: in Safari, open Share → Add to Home Screen.

Android: in Chrome, open the menu → Add to Home screen or Install app.

Search a DOI under Discover latest to add it to this device. Open Details → Propose for the shared atlas to send it to the lab queue (GitHub issue). That does not auto-curate the paper.

Export my shelf, then Import shelf on another browser to move saved / read / notes. Keep PDFs in the group Zotero collection — this site does not store publisher PDFs.

Open the 3D atlas once on Wi‑Fi. Figure 1 or the graphical abstract caches on this phone when an open copy exists. ACS paywalled papers often have neither.
